**Keeps translating when the app is off the screen, at full speed again when you come back.**

In 0.1.4 the notification stayed while you used other apps, but the translation hardly moved
until you came back to Video Translator. Android lets an app that is not on the screen use fewer
of the phone's processor cores, and the app kept working as if it still had all of them, so its
workers mostly waited for each other. Now it uses as many as Android allows at each moment:

- **Writing down what is said (Whisper):** when cores are taken away it starts again from the
  end of the last finished line, with fewer workers, so at most the last half minute of speech is
  heard twice. When you come back it takes the cores back at the next line.
- **Translating into English (the language model):** it adjusts before every word it writes.

Away from the screen it is still slower than with the app open, because Android gives it fewer
and smaller cores; how much slower depends on the phone. Keeping the app open is the fastest.

To install: download the `.apk` below on the phone and install it over 0.1.4 (same app, nothing
to load again).

Checked before release: no network permission; signed with the release key; on an emulator a
real video still becomes the right English, with the language model too; and on the PC test
machines, with cores taken away every 30 seconds and given back, the lines, their times and the
English still came out right. Not checked: on a real phone with the screen off; the emulator
cannot show that. Speed with the app open is unchanged.
