**Watch your video with the English subtitles on it.**

When a translation has finished, tap **Watch with subtitles**. The video plays in the app with the
English over it. Nothing is copied: it plays the video you picked.

- **Change how they look**, with the video in view while you do: tap **Change how the subtitles
  look** and every change shows on the video straight away (when nobody is speaking at that
  moment, the nearest line is shown so you can still see it). Size, height on the picture, text
  colour, font, bold, an outline or shadow around the letters, and a box behind the text with how
  solid it is. The look is remembered for the next video.
- **Fix the timing**: if every line comes too early or too late, move them all with -1 s, -0.1,
  +0.1 and +1 s while the video plays.
- **Long lines are split** into subtitles of at most two short lines, as on TV.
- **Save the subtitles** as a `.srt` file (any video player can show it) or as plain text, wherever
  you choose. Nothing is saved unless you do this.
- **Watch again later without waiting**: on the first screen, tap **Watch a video with subtitles
  you saved**, pick the video, then the `.srt` file you saved for it. Whisper does not run again.
- Turn the phone sideways for a bigger picture.

To install: download the `.apk` below on the phone and install it over the previous version (same
app, nothing to load again).
