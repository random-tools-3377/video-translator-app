**Open a subtitle file from My Files, or share it to the app, and put it together with its video.**

- **Tap a .srt in My Files** (or share it to Video Translator): the phone now opens it with Video
  Translator. The app says how many lines it read and asks for the video they belong to: tap
  **Pick the video**, or go to Gallery and share the video to Video Translator. The video then
  plays with those subtitles, ready to correct, restyle or save as a copy. Subtitle files from
  other apps (.vtt, .sbv) open the same way.
- **Sharing a video to the app** no longer starts translating straight away. It asks: **Translate
  it**, or **Add subtitles** (then pick the .srt or transcript you have for it).
- **Open saved subtitles or a transcript** (in the app) now asks for the subtitle file first, then
  the video.
- When the app may change the file you opened, **Save changes in (file name)** saves your
  corrections into it. A file opened from My Files usually cannot be changed by another app: then
  use **Save subtitles (.srt)** once to save a corrected copy, and later corrections go into that
  one.

Still no internet permission. The subtitle file is read once; the app keeps no copy of it.

To install: download the `.apk` below on the phone and install it over the previous version.
