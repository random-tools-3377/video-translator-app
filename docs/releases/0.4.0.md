**See all the words and correct them yourself.**

- **In the player**, tap **See and correct the words**. Every line is listed with its time, under the
  video. The line being said is highlighted as the video plays.
- **Tap a line** to hear it (the video jumps to it and plays just that line) and change it: fix the
  words, and if it shows a little too early or late, move its start or end by a tenth of a second.
  **Save and play** saves and plays it again so you can check. **Remove this line** takes out a line
  that should not be there.
- When the line was translated from Japanese, Russian or another language, the words as they were
  said are shown under the English, to help you work out what was meant.
- Your corrections are used everywhere: the subtitles in the player, a saved `.srt` or text file,
  and a copy with the subtitles burned in. You can also tap a line on the results screen, right
  after a translation, to correct it there.
- Corrections are kept only while the app is open, like the rest of the result. To keep them, save
  the subtitles (`.srt`) or a copy of the video.

To install: download the `.apk` below on the phone and install it over the previous version (same
app, nothing to load again).

Checked before release: no network permission; signed with the release key; on an emulator a real
video still becomes the right English, with the language model too, and a copy is still made with
the subtitles burned in. Not checked: the new line list and editing on a real phone (the build
machines cannot show screens).
