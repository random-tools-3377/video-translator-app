**Save a copy of the video with the subtitles in it.**

In the player, tap **Save a copy with the subtitles**. A new video is made with the English burned
into the picture, looking exactly as in the player (your size, colours, font, height and the timing
you set), and it goes to your gallery in **Movies/Video Translator**. Your original video is not
changed, and the sound is copied as it is.

- It takes a while for a long video (the whole picture is made again). A notification shows how far
  it is, with **Cancel**, and it keeps going while you use other apps.
- When it is done, tap **Open it**, or find it in your gallery.
- Like any video in your gallery, a cloud backup (Samsung Cloud, Google Photos) may upload the copy,
  subtitles included. Nothing in the app itself goes online.
- While it is being made, the phone needs free space for about two copies of the video for a
  moment; the extra one is removed straight away.

To install: download the `.apk` below on the phone and install it over the previous version (same
app, nothing to load again).
